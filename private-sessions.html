Questions.

What is a Demartini Method session?▼

The Demartini Method is a structured questioning process developed by Dr. John Demartini. In a private session, Sam guides you through precise questions about one specific person, memory, or trait until you can see it in a more balanced way. Sessions run 90 minutes to 2 hours by video.

How is this different from coaching?▼

Coaching usually focuses on goals, plans, and accountability. A Demartini Method session focuses on one perception that is holding you back and works it through in a single structured session. Many clients use it alongside coaching.

Is this therapy?▼

No. It's an educational facilitation process, not psychotherapy or counseling, and it doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. If you're in crisis or need clinical care, please see a licensed professional first.

Do I have to share my whole story?▼

No. The work focuses on how you see one person or pattern, not on retelling what happened.

Single session or program?▼

One specific issue: start with a single session. Something broader, like self-worth: the program. We'll decide together on the fit call.

How soon will I notice a difference?▼

Many clients feel a shift during or soon after a session. Results vary and aren't guaranteed.

Is it confidential?▼

Yes, under our written agreement. It isn't legally privileged, so please leave out confidential client or case details. They aren't needed.

Can I book from outside the US?▼

Yes. Sessions are by video, and Sam has worked with clients in 10 countries, including the US, Australia, the UK, Canada, and France. You choose your time zone when you book.
