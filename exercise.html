Under Your Skin

For the one person, or moment, you can’t stop replaying. Name it, get specific, and take your first clear look at it.

Most of what holds capable people back isn’t a lack of skill. It’s one thing: the boss, client, partner or coworker whose words you replay at 2 a.m., a voice of doubt, or a memory that keeps pulling you off your game.

TimeAbout 10 minutes
WhereSomewhere quiet, uninterrupted
HowYour first honest answer

Your answers stay on this device. Nothing you type here is sent to Sam or anyone else.

Step 1 · Name it

What’s pulling you off your game right now?

Choose the one that carries the most weight.

Step 2 · Get specific

Set the story aside. What exactly happened?

What exactly did they say or do? Stories keep it vague and heavy. Specifics make it something you can actually look at.

See how: from story to specific
“She’s impossible.”→ “She interrupted me twice in the meeting.”
“I’m a fraud.”→ “I freeze when someone senior asks me a question.”
“I always let people down.”→ “I missed my daughter’s recital for a client call.”
Step 3 · Score it

Right now, how one-sided does it feel?

0 means it’s pure upside. 100 means it’s all cost and no benefit. Most people start close to 100, and that’s exactly the point.
