2025–2026 Board of Directors

Meet the Board.

The people behind NSA SoCal — serving the professional speaking community across Southern California.

Board of Directors 11 members
President
Nick Demos

Tony Award Winning Broadway Producer, award-winning filmmaker, and Story Coach. Nick connects with audiences by authentically sharing compelling stories — from challenging moments to phenomenal successes — with truth and a keen sense of humor. He speaks for entrepreneurs, corporations, and financial advisors alike.

His work is helping people tap into their innate creativity and share their stories so they can connect with their clients, customers, teammates and audiences.

thenickdemos.com › president@nsasocal.org
President-Elect
Hilary Bilbrey

Hilary brings energy, empathy, and a touch of playfulness to everything she does, creating meaningful connections with audiences and teams alike. As a dynamic speaker, facilitator, and executive coach, she draws on over two decades of experience helping leaders and organizations unlock their potential.

Hilary has guided teams from conflict to collaboration, from stress to success, and from surviving to thriving.

peq-performance.com › president.elect@nsasocal.org
Immediate Past President
Dr. Sara Safari

Award-winning author, speaker, mountain climber, college professor, Electrical Engineer, and advocate for women's empowerment. Sara received the Global Citizen award from the United Nations Association in 2015 and the Outstanding Practice with Broad Impact award from the International Leadership Association in 2017.

climbyoureverest.org › past.president@nsasocal.org
Treasurer
Dawn Bloomer

Dawn began her entrepreneurial career as a veterinarian specializing in racehorses. As a business owner she grew her practice to multiple seven figures, joined forces with other practices, and was eventually acquired by a large aggregator.

In addition to her DVM and MBA, Dawn holds certifications as a Certified Exit Planning Advisor (CEPA), Certified Professional in Leadership Development (CPLD), and Certified Professional Business Coach (CPBA).

productivepressure.com › treasurer@nsasocal.org
Secretary
Dan Faill

Using humor and stories to create deeper connection with audiences, Dan makes the uncomfortable more relatable. As an accomplished storyteller and international speaker, Dan incorporates his own lived experiences, alongside industry-proven research, to craft engaging and relatable experiences for audiences.

Having spent over 15 years on college campuses, Dan now travels the country as a full-time speaker and consultant.

danfaill.com › secretary@nsasocal.org
Director of Membership
Dan Griffin

Dan has spent over 25 years helping men, fathers, and professionals do the inner work needed to heal, build real relationships, and live more fully. With 30+ years in recovery, he brings lived experience as a husband, father, and man who has faced shame, disconnection, and identity head-on.

Through his books and trainings, Dan has helped thousands move from performing to healing. He also serves on the board of White Bison, supporting Indigenous wellbriety.

dangriffin.com › membership@nsasocal.org
Co-Director, Academy
Jessper Maquindang

With over 12 years of experience leading diverse teams, Jessper understands the rewarding impact of creating workplace cultures that embrace collaboration and innovation. His passion for creative brainstorming and continuous improvement drives him to experiment with new ideas and explore better ways of doing things.

Recipient of Santa Clarita Valley's "40 Under Forty" award for significant leadership contributions to the community.

famileadconsulting.com › academy@nsasocal.org
Co-Director, Academy
Chris Holliday

Chris trains and coaches leaders, presenters, and speakers as a certified coach and facilitator. She brings passion and humor to working with executives to become the best version of themselves on stage, on camera, in the boardroom, and with their people. Her signature talk focuses on moving beyond the "Uncertainty Paradox" to lead effectively.

Chris holds an MBA and MEd and is a proud member of the Screen Actors Guild.

chrishollidaycoaching.com › academy@nsasocal.org
Director of Operations
Dr. Regina Lark

A recognized expert and energetic speaker, Dr. Regina F. Lark, Ph.D regularly educates and engages audiences at conferences and in companies on women's leadership, time management, productivity, and more.

Dr. Lark helps organizations navigate the role gender plays in employee productivity, engagement, and performance — supporting teams and individuals as they work through mental clutter to overcome procrastination and disorganization.

aclearpath.net › hello@nsasocal.org
Director of Special Events
Alina Ugas

Alina J Ugas is an experienced Executive and Life Coach, Mentor, and Hypnotherapist — the engine behind 360 Mindset Group. Her diverse background in healthcare, mental health, and business entrepreneurship offers a unique perspective to leaders and innovators across various industries.

Alina emphasizes work-life balance and time management to prevent burnout, drawing from her own experiences as a serial entrepreneur, caregiver, and parent.

360mindsetcoach.com › hello@nsasocal.org
Director of Marketing
Sam Osborne

Sam Osborne is a Demartini Method Facilitator and corporate keynote speaker specializing in conflict transformation and high-stakes leadership. He equips leaders in high-conflict environments with a precise, research-informed methodology that transforms how they perceive tension, authority, and opposition.

Rather than motivational inspiration, Sam delivers applied frameworks grounded in values hierarchy, projection mechanics, and perceptual balance — helping audiences convert reactivity into composure and conflict into data.

samosborne.net › marketing@nsasocal.org
