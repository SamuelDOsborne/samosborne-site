The Curriculum

Four weeks. Four sessions.

One move a week, applied live to your own material — so by the end you're not just resolved, you have the tool.

  1. I

    Name the Charge

    Get specific about the thing you've been calling purely negative — the guilt, the imposter feeling, the pattern you keep repeating. Vague doesn't work here. Precise does.

  2. II

    Find the Hidden Benefit

    Locate what that "flaw" has actually been doing for you all along. This is the session people describe as the "aha."

  3. III

    Balance the Two Sides

    Hold the cost and the benefit at once until neither one runs the show. This is where the charge actually lifts.

  4. IV

    Make It Stick

    Apply the same method to a second pattern on your own, with the group's support. You leave with the tool, not just one resolved issue.

Common Questions

No. It's educational, not a substitute for treatment. If you're currently in crisis, please reach out to a licensed professional first — this group is for steady, curious adults ready to do the work.
