Questions

Worth asking before you book a call

Is this therapy?

No. I'm not a licensed psychotherapist or mental health provider, and this isn't a substitute for clinical care. It's specific work on a real problem, guided by frameworks I've spent years developing — including my training as a Demartini Method Facilitator.

Do I need to already know Jung, alchemy, or the Demartini Method?

No prior study required. Bring the stuck problem, not a reading list.

What actually happens in a session?

Ninety minutes, live, with a small group. Each session works a real, current problem someone in the room brought.

What if I miss a week?

Say so ahead of time where you can. A single missed session isn't a problem, and I'll help you catch the thread.

Is what I share kept private?

What's said in the group stays in the group — no exceptions.

What if it's not the right fit after the first session?

First session, no questions, money back. The short call before you commit exists specifically to make this unlikely, but the option stays open regardless.

Why is it priced this low for what's included?

This is the first cohort, priced accordingly. No bonuses, no stacked "value" — just the honest cost of a small group, direct access, and a program I'm running for the first time in this form.
