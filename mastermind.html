Jung called it the transcendent function: a third position, arising from two opposites held in genuine tension, that neither side could have produced alone. This is where that function gets exercised — deliberately, on your own material.

Why This Study Exists

Most people treat a dream as noise. It is closer to correspondence.

The recurring dream, the symbol you keep meeting, the argument between who you are and who you're becoming — these are not distractions from the work. In this tradition, they are the work, and they respond to structured attention.

Dream Work as Data

Members bring live dream material into every session. It is read symbolically, alchemically, and personally — not decoded, but held.

Opposites Held in Tension

The method starts by locating a genuine pair of opposites in your own life — not resolving them prematurely, but holding both with equal weight.

A Monthly Practice

Not a single workshop and not an open-ended group. A fixed cadence, a small roster, and a curriculum that moves in a genuine sequence.

The Six-Month Study

Each monthly session works one stage of the transcendent function in sequence — read from primary material, then applied live to something you bring: a dream, a recurring symbol, a tension you can't resolve by thinking harder about it.

You are never asked to interpret someone else's dream, and no one is asked to explain their inner life to the group beyond what they choose to share. The structure is shared; the material is your own.

Sessions run 90 minutes over video, capped at twelve members, with primary-text reading guides and a private thread for the weeks in between.

  1. Reading a Dream as Data

    Moving from "what does this mean" to a disciplined method of amplification and personal association.

  2. Locating the Opposites

    Finding the genuine tension underneath a symptom, a recurring image, or a stuck decision.

  3. Active Imagination

    Holding both poles in imaginal contact, without collapsing toward either one, until something moves.

  4. The Transcendent Function

    Where the third position — genuinely new, not a compromise — begins to appear.

  5. Nigredo, Albedo, Rubedo

    The alchemical stages of the work, and how to recognize which one you're standing in.

  6. Mercurius and the Symbol That Won't Resolve

    What to do with the material that refuses a tidy ending — and why that's often the point.

Before You Apply

The two questions worth asking first.

“Isn't this just journaling with extra steps?”

Journaling records the material. This works it. Active imagination and the amplification method are structured techniques for holding a symbol or a tension until something genuinely new emerges — a different outcome than writing something down and moving on.

“Do I need a background in Jung to join?”

No. Members range from long-time students of the esoteric to people who've never opened a Jung text but can't shake a recurring dream. The reading guides carry the terminology; you bring the material and the willingness to sit with it.

Common Questions

What to expect.

What if I don't remember my dreams?▾

Recall is a skill that develops with the practice, not a prerequisite for it. The first month covers a simple method for improving recall, and the curriculum works just as well with a waking symbol, a recurring image, or a decision you can't resolve rationally.

Is this therapy or mental health treatment?▾

No. This is an educational and developmental study group in the Jungian tradition, not psychotherapy, counseling, or medical care. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and is not a substitute for licensed care. If you are dealing with a mental health condition, please work with a qualified provider alongside this practice, not instead of it.

How is this different from a book club?▾

A book club discusses the text. This applies it. Every session includes live, structured facilitation on material a member brings — a dream, a symbol, a tension — using the amplification and active imagination methods the reading covers.

What's the group size and how is confidentiality handled?▾

Cohorts are capped at twelve members (six for the Inner Circle). Everyone agrees to hold what's shared in session as confidential. You're never required to share more of your material than you choose to.

What's the time commitment?▾

One 90-minute live session a month, plus whatever time you give to your own dream journal and the reading guide between sessions. There's no daily obligation.

Can I join without prior background in Jung or the esoteric traditions?▾

Yes. The reading guides are written to carry the terminology as the cohort goes. What matters more is a genuine willingness to bring real material and sit with it past the point of an easy answer.
