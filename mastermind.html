Common Questions

What to expect.

Is this therapy?+

No. It's educational, not a substitute for treatment. If you're currently in crisis, please reach out to a licensed professional first — this group is for steady, curious adults ready to do the work.

What exactly does a session look like?+

You bring one specific thing — a pattern, a person, a piece of guilt. Sam walks you through a fixed set of questions live, in front of the group, until you can see what that thing has actually been doing for you.

Do I need experience with this kind of work?+

No. Just a willingness to look at something you've already decided is a flaw.

Is this confidential?+

Yes. This is a small, closed room — not a recorded webinar shared publicly. What's worked through in the day stays with the people in it.

What if I have to step out during the day?+

The full day is recorded. If you miss a stretch, you won't fall behind.

What's the group size?+

Small and capped, on purpose — everyone gets worked live, not just talked at.

Has this exact retreat run before?+

No — this is the first time The Ordered Field has run as a live, small-group retreat. The method has been used one-on-one for years, which is where the testimonials above come from; the retreat format itself is new, and the founding rate reflects that.
