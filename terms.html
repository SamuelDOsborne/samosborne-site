Terms of Engagement

The short version

  • Sessions are educational facilitation using The Demartini Method®. They are not therapy, counseling, or medical care.
  • Every engagement starts with a free 20-minute fit call. There’s no obligation to book.
  • Clear-Shift Guarantee: if you don’t feel a clear shift by the end of your first session, tell me before we hang up and I’ll refund you in full.

  • What you share stays confidential, with the narrow exceptions below.

1. Who these terms are between

These terms are between you and Sam Osborne (“Sam,” “I,” “me”), a trained Demartini Method Facilitator based in Los Angeles, California. They apply to private sessions booked through samosborne.net. When you book a Breakthrough Session, you agree to these terms at booking. For the Six-Session Program, I’ll also send you a short written client agreement that repeats them; if the two ever differ, the signed agreement applies. Leadership advisory and speaking engagements are covered by their own written agreements.

2. What a session is, and what it isn’t

A session is a structured, one-to-one questioning process, by video, based on The Demartini Method®, created by Dr. John F. Demartini. Each session works on one person you can’t stop reacting to, until you can see them in a more balanced way.

It is educational facilitation. It is not psychotherapy, counseling, medical care, or legal or financial advice, and it doesn’t diagnose or treat any condition. It isn’t a substitute for professional care, and you remain responsible for your own decisions and actions.

Sessions aren’t suitable if you’re in crisis, need clinical care, or are being harmed by the person you want to work on. Please start with a licensed professional or local support services. If you’re in immediate danger, call 911 (in the US) or your local emergency number.

3. The fit call

The fit call is free, takes about 20 minutes by video or phone, and carries no obligation. You’ll talk with me directly. If a session isn’t the right fit for you, I’ll say so.

4. Sessions and fees

All prices are in US dollars.

  • Breakthrough Session: $1,100. One private session of 90 minutes to 2 hours, plus a check-in about a week later. If you start the Six-Session Program within 30 days, the full $1,100 is credited toward it.
  • Six-Session Program: $4,500. Six private sessions over about three months, with message support between sessions and priority scheduling. Program sessions are to be used within four months of your first session.

5. Payment

Every session is paid for in full before it happens. I don’t invoice after a session or run my own payment plans.

  • Breakthrough Session: paid when you book your time.
  • Six-Session Program: paid in full at checkout before your first session. If you’d like to spread the cost, you can choose Klarna at checkout, subject to Klarna’s approval. I’m paid in full, and you repay Klarna under its terms. Your agreement for that is with Klarna, not me.

6. The Clear-Shift Guarantee

Your first session comes with a full money-back guarantee.

  • What it covers: your first session with me, whether that’s a Breakthrough Session or the first session of the Six-Session Program.
  • How it works: if, by the end of that session, you don’t feel a clear shift in how you see the person we worked on, tell me before the session ends. I’ll refund everything you’ve paid, in full, with no further questions.
  • On the program: the program is cancelled and the full $4,500 is refunded. If you paid with Klarna, the refund goes back through Klarna, which adjusts or closes your plan.
  • Timing: refunds go back to your original payment method within 7 business days.
  • Conditions: the guarantee applies once per client, and it requires that you attend the full session and answer the questions in good faith. It must be claimed before the session ends; it doesn’t apply to later sessions.

7. Rescheduling and cancellations

  • You can reschedule for free with at least 24 hours’ notice.
  • With less than 24 hours’ notice, or if you don’t attend, the session counts as used. I may waive this once for a genuine emergency.
  • If I need to reschedule, you can choose a new time or a full refund for that session.
  • If you arrive late, the session still ends at its scheduled time.

8. Refunds outside the guarantee

Apart from the Clear-Shift Guarantee and sessions I cancel, fees aren’t refundable once a session has taken place. If you decide to stop the Six-Session Program after your first session, unused sessions aren’t refunded, but you have four months from your first session to use them.

9. Confidentiality

What you share in sessions stays confidential. I won’t disclose it without your written permission, except where the law requires it or where I believe there is a serious and imminent risk of harm to you or someone else.

Sessions aren’t legally privileged, so please don’t share confidential client, case, or employer information. It isn’t needed. Sessions aren’t recorded unless we both agree in writing.

10. Testimonials

I’ll only publish what you say about a session, and only in the form you approve, with your written permission. You can withdraw that permission at any time.

12. Limitation of liability

To the extent the law allows, my total liability for any claim relating to a session is limited to the amount you paid for that session, and I’m not liable for indirect or consequential losses.

13. Governing law and disputes

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of California. If something goes wrong, please contact me first so we can try to resolve it directly.
