How a Session Works

Precise. Structured. Yours.

One Focus, Worked Through Completely

Each session takes one specific person, memory, or trait and works it through from start to finish. You don't need to retell your whole history. The method focuses on how you perceive what happened, not on reliving it.

Sam guides you through a precise sequence of questions. You find the answers yourself. That is why the shift tends to last: nothing is imposed on you.

Sessions run by video, typically 90 minutes to 2 hours, so the work is never rushed.

The Shift Inside Each Session

  • Name the specific person, memory, or trait carrying the charge
  • Score how one-sided it currently feels
  • Look at what you've been unable to see on the other side
  • Connect it to what you value most
  • Bring your perception into balance
  • Leave with clarity you can feel, and test it in real life

Why Sam

The person asking the questions.

In 2008, a piece of metal tore through Sam's left eye and pierced his retina. His eyesight recovered. His relationship with perception never did.

He was left with a question he couldn't put down: if the way we see can be damaged and repaired, what else is it quietly doing that nobody notices? That question led him to the Demartini Method in 2014, and to the idea at the center of his work: the size of a reaction is a reliable measure of what a person can't yet see. Balance the seeing, and the reaction doesn't need managing.

Since then he has guided nearly 200 hours of private sessions for speakers, coaches, leaders, and professionals in 10 countries. Many first knew him as “Self Worth Sam.” He also demonstrates the process live on stage for attorneys, advisors, and executives.

Born in Perth, Australia and now based in Los Angeles, Sam holds a double major in Philosophy and English and is the author of six books. He is direct, and if a session isn't right for you, he'll say so on the fit call.

Common Questions

What to expect.

Is this therapy or mental health treatment?▼

No. The Demartini Method is a structured, educational facilitation process, not psychotherapy or counseling. It does not diagnose or treat any medical, psychological, or mental health condition, and it is not a substitute for care from a licensed mental health professional. If you are dealing with a mental health condition, please work with a qualified provider. This work can sit alongside that care, not replace it.

Do I have to share my whole story?▼

No. The method works on how you perceive a specific person, memory, or trait, not on retelling everything that happened. Many clients are surprised by how little background is needed.

Should I start with a single session or the program?▼

If there's one specific person or situation weighing on you, start with a Breakthrough Session. If the issue is broader, such as self-worth or a pattern that shows up across your life and work, the Six-Session Program gives us room to work through each piece. We'll decide together on the fit call, and a single session can be credited toward the program if you continue within 30 days.

How quickly will I notice a difference?▼

Many clients report a noticeable shift during or shortly after a session. Individual experiences vary, and no specific result or timeframe is guaranteed. The practical test is how you feel the next time you face that person, moment, or decision.

What happens during a session?▼

You name the person, memory, or trait carrying the charge and score how one-sided it feels. I then guide you through a precise sequence of questions designed to bring your perception into balance. You do the discovering; I keep the process on track.

How is confidentiality handled?▼

Sessions are private, and your information is kept confidential under the terms of our written agreement. This is not a legally privileged relationship, so please don't share confidential client or case details. They aren't needed for the work.

Who is this not right for?▼

If you are in crisis, or managing a condition that needs clinical care, please contact a licensed professional or your local emergency services first. This work is best suited to people who are functioning well overall and want to resolve a specific perception that's holding them back.
