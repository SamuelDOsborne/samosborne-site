About Sam

A Demartini Method Facilitator. A keynote speaker. A very unusual career path.

Sam Osborne has been a school teacher, human beatbox entertainer, events DJ, living statue, and kitchen tiler. A piece of metal once lodged itself in his retina. He recovered. His eyesight recovered… mostly. His relationship with perception did not — it transformed.

He spent years trying to understand why. Then he found the Demartini Method — and the transformation became teachable. He's been speaking about it ever since.

When the trigger owns you, the room does too. Sam works with attorneys, executives, and association professionals who operate in high-conflict environments — people who are sharp, capable, and occasionally one difficult colleague away from saying something they can't take back.

Six books. A double major in Philosophy and English. And yes — he will connect it back to Carl Jung.

By day he is Director of Marketing for a commercial roofing company — which, it turns out, is excellent training for finding hidden order in chaos. Originally from Perth, Western Australia. Now based in Los Angeles. Board Member, National Speakers Association Southern California.
