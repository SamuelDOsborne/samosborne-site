The Show

Composure isn’t a talent.
It’s a mechanism.

Each episode takes one real trigger — a hostile deposition, a difficult client, a colleague who won’t let something go — and runs it through the same process used live on stage: name the behavior, measure the imbalance, get your line of sight back. No general advice to “stay calm.” A repeatable mechanism, built for rooms where losing composure has a real cost.

The Format

Four steps.
Every episode.

The same arc every time, so the mechanism becomes recognizable — and usable — long before the episode ends.

01 — THE BLUR

The trigger

The moment described specifically — a phrase, a look, an interruption. Not a general complaint.

02 — THE LENS

The concept

Why it landed — values hierarchy, projection, perceptual balance — applied to this exact situation.

03 — THE FOCUS

The imbalance

Named and measured — the 100/0 moment, made visible instead of just felt.

04 — LINE OF SIGHT

The adjustment

One question the listener can ask themselves next time it happens — on Monday, in the room.

Be a Guest

Bring a real trigger.
Leave with your line of sight back.

Guest episodes are built for the show's actual audience — attorneys, advisors, executives, and sales leaders — not other speakers or coaches. You bring one real, specific trigger; Sam runs it through the same four-step process used live on stage, on mic.

  • No prep required — the trigger just needs to be real and specific
  • Nothing private or dramatic is required — a behavior and a name is enough
  • You leave with your own episode to share with your network
  • Recorded remotely, roughly 30–40 minutes

You'll hear back within a few business days.
