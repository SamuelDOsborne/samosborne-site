The Session

What This Is

The Problem

Emotional reactivity distorts judgment under pressure. Professionals in high-conflict environments are rarely taught why certain triggers produce disproportionate reactions.

The Solution

A structured perceptual process — delivered by an attorney and a Demartini Method facilitator — that dissolves charge at the source. You'll see it work in real time, on a live volunteer.

What Leaders Handle Differently Monday

Concrete Behaviors. Not Concepts.

Identify

Why specific people trigger charge — and trace it to its source

Apply

A self-governance process in real-time, even mid-conversation

Reframe

Conflict as data rather than threat — changing decisions that follow

Communicate

With precision under pressure, reducing reactive escalation

Lead

With composure in destabilizing environments, without suppressing

Return

To the process independently — they leave with a method, not a feeling
