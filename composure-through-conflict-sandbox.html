The Session

What This Program Addresses

The Problem

Emotional reactivity under pressure distorts judgment, damages relationships, and erodes credibility. Most conflict training teaches communication techniques. This program goes earlier in the chain — to the perception underneath behavior.

The Solution

A structured perceptual methodology delivered by an attorney and a Demartini Method facilitator. Includes a live volunteer demonstration so your audience witnesses the methodology working in real time — not described, but seen.

Monday Morning Behaviors

What Leaders Handle Differently

Identify Triggers

Why specific people and situations produce charge — and trace it to its source

Apply In Real-Time

A self-governance process mid-conversation, even in high-stakes moments

Reframe Conflict

As data rather than threat — changing the decisions that follow

Communicate Precisely

Under pressure, reducing ambiguity and reactive escalation

Lead With Composure

In destabilizing environments, without suppressing or performing

Return Independently

To the process on their own — they leave with method, not just feeling
