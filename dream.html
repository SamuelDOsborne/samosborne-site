Questions seekers ask

I don’t remember my dreams well. Can I still come?

Yes. One dream is enough — even a fragment. You’ll also learn simple ways to remember more in the weeks before the day.

Do I need to know astrology or Jung?

No. Everything is taught from the ground up. You’ll just need your birth date, place, and exact birth time to the minute — a birth certificate or hospital record is the best source. Without an accurate time, the chart can’t be read properly.

Will my dream be shared with the group?

Only what you choose to share. The circle is small and held with care; you can work privately during any exercise.

Why an application?

To keep the circle small and right for the work — and to make sure this is a good fit for where you are right now.

Is this therapy?

No. It is an educational study day in dream interpretation and self-reflection, not psychotherapy or medical care.
