The Formats

Where the keynote opens the door, these formats walk the room through it.

Each format is designed to be defended in a budget conversation: clear deliverables, defined audience capacity, and an outcome that survives Monday morning.

Half-Day Workshop

Workshop with Live Demartini Method Demonstration

A three-to-four hour working session built around a live Demartini Method demonstration with a volunteer from the room. Attendees don't just hear the framework — they watch an emotional charge actually dissolve in real time, on a real conflict from a real leader's life. That demonstration is the engine of the workshop.

Around it, Sam teaches the perceptual balance process step by step, walks the room through paired application on their own current triggers, and equips each attendee with a worksheet they can use the following Monday morning.

Duration
3–4 hours
Ideal For
Pre-conference intensive, leadership retreat anchor session, or half-day deep-dive following the signature keynote.
Audience
150 for full participation · 300 for demonstration-and-teach format
Includes
Live demonstration · Step-by-step teaching · Paired application · Take-home worksheet

Outcome Attendees leave with a repeatable method, not a memory of a good talk.
Multi-Session Intensive

Executive Retreats & Leadership Intensives

A multi-session program designed for leadership teams, executive cohorts, and association boards that need depth rather than exposure. Built across two to four sessions over one to three days, the intensive moves a defined group through all four pillars: The Conflict Advantage, The Admiration Trap, The Defended Self, and The Disowned Self.

Each session pairs a teaching block with live facilitation, structured paired work, and integration time so the framework lands in the actual relationships people lead.

Duration
2–4 sessions over 1–3 days
Ideal For
Executive offsites, partner retreats, board development weekends, and high-stakes leadership cohorts where the same people will keep working together long after the program ends.
Audience
12–40 for full immersion
Includes
All four pillars · Live facilitation · Structured paired work · Integration time · Pre-program design with planner
Investment
From $75,000
Outcome A measurably calmer, clearer, more honest leadership culture.
