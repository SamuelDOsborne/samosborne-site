Extended Formats

For audiences that need the method, not just the framework.

The keynote opens the door. These formats walk the room through it — with live facilitation, guided application, and time enough for the work to actually land.

Half-Day Workshop

Workshop with Live Demartini Method Demonstration

A three-to-four hour working session built around a live Demartini Method demonstration with a volunteer from the room. Attendees don't just hear the framework — they watch an emotional charge actually dissolve in real time, on a real conflict from a real leader's life. That demonstration is the engine of the workshop.

Around it, Sam teaches the perceptual balance process step by step, walks the room through guided partner work on their own current triggers, and equips each attendee with a worksheet they can use the following Monday morning.

Duration 3–4 hours
Audience 150 full participation · 300 demo-and-teach
Ideal For Pre-conference intensive, retreat anchor session, half-day deep-dive following the keynote
Investment From $40,000
Outcome Attendees leave with a repeatable method, not a memory of a good talk.
Multi-Session Intensive

Executive Retreats & Leadership Intensives

A multi-session program designed for leadership teams, executive cohorts, and association boards that need depth rather than exposure. Built across two to four sessions over one to three days, the intensive moves a defined group through all four pillars: The Conflict Advantage, The Admiration Trap, The Defended Self, and The Disowned Self.

Each session pairs a teaching block with live facilitation, structured partner work, and integration time so the framework lands in the actual relationships people lead.

Duration 2–4 sessions over 1–3 days
Audience 12–40 for full immersion
Ideal For Executive offsites, partner retreats, board development weekends, leadership cohorts
Investment From $75,000
Outcome A measurably calmer, clearer, more honest leadership culture.
