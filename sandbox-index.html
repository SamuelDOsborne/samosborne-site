Common Questions

How is this different from executive coaching?

Executive coaching typically focuses on skill development, behavioral modification, or goal achievement. This is perceptual transformation. The Demartini Method dissolves the emotional charge at the root of reactivity. You don't learn techniques to manage emotions around difficult people. You permanently transform how you perceive them. When perception balances, the charge disappears.

What if I need more than 8 or 16 sessions?

Most leaders find that 8–16 facilitations address their highest-priority charged relationships. If additional sessions are required after completing your initial engagement, we can discuss extension options. The goal is permanent transformation, not ongoing dependency.

How quickly will I see results?

Perceptual balance occurs during the facilitation session itself. You'll notice the shift immediately—the person who previously triggered intense reactivity no longer carries the same emotional charge. The composure you demonstrate in the next interaction confirms the transformation.

What happens during a session?

Each session is a structured facilitation. You identify the person and the trait that triggers charge. You’re guided through precision questions that reveal where you display that same trait. Through reflective awareness and perceptual balance, projection mechanics dissolve. By the end of the session, the emotional charge around that person is gone.

Is this appropriate for organizational teams?

This advisory service is for individual leaders. If you're interested in applying the Demartini Method to team dynamics or organizational conflict, that requires a different engagement structure. Contact me to discuss organizational consulting options.

Do I need to have attended one of your keynotes?

No. While many advisory clients are keynote attendees who witnessed the live demonstration, the engagement is open to any leader experiencing emotional reactivity toward specific people that undermines authority. The consultation call will confirm fit.
