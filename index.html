The Demonstration

Most speakers explain composure. Sam dissolves
a real conflict on stage.

Not a role-play. A volunteer names someone who genuinely gets under their skin. Minutes later, the charge is gone. Not managed. Gone.

Every format. Including the keynote.

01

The drama comes out

She's impossible. He undermined me. The story is where the charge hides, so it goes first.

02

The behavior gets named

He interrupted twice. Something small and observable. A behavior, not a villain.

03

The imbalance gets a number

How one-sided does it feel? Usually 100% cost, 0% benefit. Now the room has a baseline.

The number moves

A handful of questions, no advice. The volunteer revises it themselves, out loud.

About Sam

The injury was 2008. The method came six years later.

Sam Osborne helps nurse leaders, healthcare executives and care teams stay calm and think clearly when a frightened family turns on them, a colleague snaps, or a patient panics.

In 2008 a piece of metal pierced his retina. He knows what it is to be the frightened patient your staff meet every day, and it left him with a question he couldn’t put down: what else is perception quietly doing that nobody notices?

His answer: the size of a reaction is a reliable measure of what a person can’t yet see. Balance the seeing, and there’s nothing left to manage.

He is not a motivational speaker, and says so before anyone else can. People use it the next day, or it didn’t work.

The Method

A repeatable framework.
Not a motivational talk.

Three steps, used in order. Built to change what people do on Monday, not how they feel for an hour. From the Demartini Method; Sam is a Trained Demartini Method Facilitator.

The Demartini Method, developed by Dr. John Demartini, is a human transformation methodology designed to transform human perceptions. It is being used professionally by many psychologists, psychiatrists, social workers, educators, consultants and health professionals across the world. The Demartini Method is a tool for empowering and inspiring an individual’s life, and its applications include reducing stress, resolving conflict, and creating new perspectives and paradigms for life. Sam’s programs are educational and developmental. They are not clinical or mental-health treatment.

Before You Book

The questions chairs actually ask.

What if nobody volunteers?

The ask is small: name a person and one behavior, nothing private. A backup volunteer is agreed with the host in advance, so it runs either way.

What if the volunteer doesn't shift?

The number belongs to the volunteer, and a partial shift still teaches the mechanism. An honest change is more persuasive than a clean one.

Is this safe for a professional audience?

Yes. The method strips the drama out and works on observable behavior, so the volunteer is never exposed. A safe space to get there, as one attendee put it.

Will this work for clinicians?

Yes. Clinicians are trained to stay calm, which is why the moments they can’t cost so much. Examples come from your units, and no patient information is ever needed.
