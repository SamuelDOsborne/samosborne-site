The Demartini Method

Developed by Dr. John Demartini, this research-informed, systematic methodology grounded in established principles of psychology, perception, and values reveals how conflict, infatuation, and resentment arise from imbalanced perception — and how reflective awareness restores equilibrium. Sam applies this framework to high-stakes leadership challenges.

Perceptual Balance

Every person, event, and challenge contains equal parts support and challenge. When you develop the precision to see both sides, emotional reactivity dissolves. This is the foundation of conflict transformation — not by avoiding difficulty, but by seeing it completely.

Values Hierarchy

Conflict often signals misaligned values. Your values hierarchy determines what you care about most, what you'll fight for, and what you'll ignore. When you lead from your highest values — not borrowed values — internal and external conflict diminish dramatically.

Projection & Injection

The people who trigger you most reveal what you haven't owned in yourself. Through reflective awareness, you reclaim these traits in your own form. When projection dissolves, so does the emotional charge that fuels conflict and undermines composure.
