Try one right now

The mirror question.

The trait you can’t stand in someone close to you is often a part of yourself you haven’t made peace with. This exercise takes five minutes and changes how that person feels to you.

Step 1

Think of someone close who frustrates you. Name the one trait that bothers you most.

Step 2

Where have you shown that same trait, even once, even in a different form? Write down three moments.

Step 3

For each moment, who did it help or protect? Notice how your feeling toward them, and toward yourself, begins to soften.
