Privacy Policy

Last updated October 31, 2026

The short version

  • I collect only what you give me in forms and bookings, and I use it to reply to you, book sessions, and send occasional notes.
  • Your answers in the free exercise are saved on your own device, not by me.
  • I don’t sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • You can ask me to see, correct, or delete your information at any time.

1. Who I am

This site is run by Sam Osborne, a trained Demartini Method Facilitator and keynote speaker based in Los Angeles, California. Contact: sam@samosborne.net.

2. What I collect

  • Fit call requests: your name, email, phone number (optional), time zone, the session you’re interested in, who’s getting under your skin (as a category), a sentence about what you’d like to work on, and how you heard about me.
  • Free exercise sign-up: your first name and email address.
  • Bookings: when you pick a time, Calendly collects your booking details on my behalf.
  • Emails and messages you send me.
  • Session notes: if you become a client, I may keep brief private notes of our sessions.

Please don’t include confidential client, case, or employer information, or sensitive health details, in forms. They aren’t needed.

3. The free Under-Your-Skin Exercise

Your answers in the exercise are saved in your own browser on your device, not on my servers, so you can come back to them. Tap “Start over” or clear your browser data to remove them.

If you tap “See examples,” the text you’ve typed is sent through this site’s server to an AI provider (Anthropic) to write examples for your situation. I don’t log or store that text. Anthropic processes it under its own commercial terms and privacy policy. If you’d rather not send it, simply don’t use “See examples”; the exercise works without it.

4. How I use your information

  • To reply to you and arrange your fit call and sessions.
  • To send occasional notes if you sign up for the free exercise. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To run sessions and keep my business and tax records.

I don’t sell or rent your information, and I don’t share it for advertising.

5. Services I use

I share information only with the services that run this site and my practice, and only so they can do that job:

  • Formspree: receives website form submissions.
  • Calendly: scheduling.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the site and serves videos, and provides Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies or tracking you across sites.
  • Anthropic: writes examples in the free exercise, as described above.
  • Google Workspace: email and records.
  • Embedded media: YouTube and Spotify players appear on some pages. They may set their own cookies when you press play.

6. How long I keep it

I keep enquiries and email sign-ups until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete them. I keep client and payment records for as long as I need them for my practice and as tax law requires, usually up to seven years.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask me to show you, correct, or delete the information I hold about you, or to stop emailing you, at any time. Email sam@samosborne.net and I’ll respond within 30 days. California residents have these rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, and clients in the UK and EU have similar rights under data protection law. I won’t treat you differently for using them.

8. Security

I use reputable services and keep access to client information limited to me. No method of sending or storing information online is completely secure, so please keep sensitive details out of forms.

9. Children

This site and my sessions are for adults. I don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

10. Changes

If I change this policy, I’ll update the date at the top of this page.
