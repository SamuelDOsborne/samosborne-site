My story

Twenty-five years of studying the soul. Then my dreams told me to write it down.

I · The shard

A shard of metal pierced my retina, and I came close to losing my sight.

My sight came back. The way I see the world never did.

II · The book

I was twelve when my father handed me a copy of Parker’s Astrology.

I didn’t know he was handing me a map. For more than twenty-five years I followed it — through Jung, Hermeticism, Kabbalah and the old esoteric traditions — on my own time, mostly in private.

III · The detours

I failed English. Then I earned a double major in it, alongside philosophy.

I’ve been a teacher, a human beatboxer, a living statue, an events DJ and a kitchen tiler. Every road kept circling the same question: what is really happening beneath the surface of a life?

IV · The drawer

In Los Angeles, I built a speaking career on composure under conflict.

The dream work stayed in a drawer. I told myself it would dilute the brand. The truth was simpler: I already knew where my heart was. I was just deferring it.

V · The messenger

In June 2026, my dreams began arriving as a series.

One shadowed figure kept returning — and kept asking for the same thing.

Record the work.

VI · The practice

So I do. Every morning, I draw the night’s dream in pencil at a wooden desk.

I read it through Jung’s lens. I hold it against my natal chart. Then I use the Demartini Method on whoever the dream has put in front of me — until the charge is gone and gratitude is what’s left.

VII · The return

This page is the record — and an open door.

What began as a private practice has become a way to master every area of my life. Here is the method, and the ways you can walk it with me.

Questions

I don’t remember my dreams well. Can I still start?

Yes. One dream is enough — even a fragment. The free checklist is the best place to begin.

Do I need to know astrology or Jung?

No. Everything is taught from the ground up. For chart work you’ll need your birth date, place, and exact birth time to the minute — a birth certificate or hospital record is the best source.

Which offer is right for me?

Start with the free checklist. If you want to go deeper at your own pace, take a course. If you want your own dream read live, join the seminar. If one person or situation is weighing on you, book a private session.

Will my dream be shared with the group?

Only what you choose to share. The seminar circle is small and held with care.

Is this therapy?

No. This is educational work in dream interpretation and self-reflection, not psychotherapy or medical care. If you are in crisis, please work with a licensed clinician first.
