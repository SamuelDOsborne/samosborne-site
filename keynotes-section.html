Conflict & Composure

Composure Through Conflict

How to Stop Taking Things So Personally

Perfect For:

Legal associations · Property management · Community leadership · High-accountability professionals in conflict-rich environments

Audiences will learn how to:

  • Identify the science behind emotional reactions and brain response
  • Separate facts from reactions in high-pressure conversations
  • Use reflective awareness to stay grounded and professional
  • Exhibit emotional discipline during difficult interactions
  • Lead with credibility and consistency when conflict is unavoidable
What makes this different: This isn't about avoiding conflict—it's about managing it with composure, credibility, and confidence. Delivered by Sam Osborne and attorney Dave Graf, this presentation combines neuroscience, practical tools, and real-world legal scenarios.
