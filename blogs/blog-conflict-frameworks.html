Why Conflict is Inevitable (and That's Good)

h2>

Conflict isn't something to eliminate—it's something to transform. The teams and organizations with the healthiest cultures aren't those without conflict; they're those with the skills to navigate it constructively.

Framework 1: The Perspective Shift Model

This framework teaches teams to see conflict as a difference in perspective rather than a personal attack. When two people disagree, instead of fighting for who's "right," ask: "What does the other person see that I'm missing?"

  • Separate the person from the perspective
  • Seek to understand before being understood
  • Look for the validity in opposing views

Framework 2: The Interest-Based Negotiation Approach

Most conflicts focus on positions, not interests. Someone wants to do X, another wants Y. But beneath positions lie shared interests. By identifying these common ground areas, solutions emerge naturally.

Framework 3: The Values-Aligned Resolution Process

The most sustainable resolutions honor everyone's core values. This framework guides teams to identify what matters most to each person and craft solutions that respect those values.
