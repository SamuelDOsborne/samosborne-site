About the Show

Turn chaos into pattern

Underneath the chaos of ordinary life, there's an order — it doesn't announce itself on schedule, but once you learn to look for it, you start seeing it everywhere. Field Notes draws on dreams, the transcendent function, Jungian psychology, and Hermetic tradition, but treats all of it as data, not mystery: a set of tools for reading your own patterns, not someone else's theory. Every episode is built so you can check the pattern against your own experience by the time it ends.

Before You Press Play

For the skeptical and the curious

Do I need to believe in the transcendent function to get anything out of this?

No. The show treats the transcendent function and dream symbols as a symbolic language for describing patterns — not as prediction. Most episodes work fine if you stay agnostic about the mechanism and just watch whether the pattern holds.

Is this therapy, or a substitute for it?

No. Field Notes is educational and reflective, not clinical. If you're working through something acute, a licensed therapist is the right first call — this show is a companion to that work, not a replacement for it.

I've never studied any of this. Where do I start?

Episode 1 and Episode 2, in that order. They're built for a first-time listener and set up the vocabulary the rest of the show leans on.
