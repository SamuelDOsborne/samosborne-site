Before you start

Most of what holds capable people back isn’t a lack of skill. It’s one person whose words you replay at 2 a.m. This exercise turns the label you’ve given them into one action a camera could record, then helps you find that same action in your own life, and what it has given you.

TimeAbout 15 minutes
WhereSomewhere quiet, uninterrupted
HowA pen, one person, one moment. Try each step before you read the examples.
1

Name them

Who’s getting under your skin right now? Pick the one person who carries the most charge.

A family memberMy partner Someone at workA client or customer A friendSomeone else
In one sentence, what’s going on:
2

Who

Who is it? First name or initials.

One person, not a group. If you wrote “people,” “my clients,” or “lawyers,” pick one person and one moment.

Who:
Examples
  • My manager, Priya
  • My husband, Dave
  • My brother Mark
3

Say it your way

What did they do, in your own words? Labels welcome here.

Circle any verdicts like manipulative, dismissed, selfish, rude, toxic, liar, fraud, controlling, always, never, “made me feel.” These are verdicts. We’ll find the action under them next.
Examples
  • “My boss strung me along.”
  • “My husband never listens.”
  • “My mum is so critical.”

4

The literal line

What exactly did they say or do? The words, or what a camera would have caught.

Still see a label? Labels aren’t endpoints. What would a camera have caught? Swap “always” or “never” for one time.

From label to literal
“My boss strung me along”He said, “You’ll make partner next year.” A year later, he didn’t raise it. “She dismissed me”She looked at her phone while I was presenting. “My husband never listens”He kept watching the game while I told him about my day. “My mum is critical”She said, “Are you really wearing that?”
5

Observed or reported?

I saw or heard it myself (live or on video) I was told or read about it

Reported actions carry less weight. If you have a moment you witnessed directly, use that one.

Examples
  • Observed: “I was in the meeting.” “I was at the dinner table.”
  • Reported: “A colleague told me.” “My sister told me.” “I saw it in a group chat.”
6

What still stings

Which part carries the charge? Underline it in step 4, or write it here.

The first label often isn’t the real trigger.

ExampleIn “You’ll make partner next year,” the charge might sit in the promise itself, the confidence it was said with, or the year of silence that followed.

Your Motor Function

Say the action in 3–4 words, starting with a verb.

One word per box. A physical action, not a verdict.

Could a camera record this? ☐ Yes    ☐ Not yet: picture the moment on video. What do their hands, face, or mouth do? Start with that verb.

From label to Motor Function

Family/Domestic: “I’ll be home for dinner,” then stayed late · Told your partner “This weekend I’ll fix it”

Social: “Let’s get coffee soon” and never sent a date · RSVP’d yes and didn’t go

Physical: Told your doctor “I’ll cut back,” then didn’t

Tightest matches have the same order: you promised first, to get a yes, then didn’t deliver.

The other side

From the moment you saw them do it, how has it served you?

  • What has this pushed you to learn, build, or become?
  • Where has it made you stronger, sharper, or more determined?
  • If a close friend had lived this, what would it have given them?
