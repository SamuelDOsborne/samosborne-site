The demonstration

Most speakers explain composure. Sam dissolves a real conflict on stage.

Not a role-play. It can’t be scripted and it can’t be faked. Your audience watches it work on a colleague.

01
The drama comes out

A volunteer names someone who genuinely gets under their skin. The story goes first.

02
The behavior gets named

Something small and observable. A behavior, not a villain. No patient information needed.

03
The imbalance gets a number

How one-sided does it feel? Usually 100% cost, 0% benefit.

04
The number moves

A handful of questions, no advice. The volunteer revises it themselves, out loud, toward 50 / 50.

About Sam

He was the frightened patient once.

In 2008 a piece of metal pierced Sam’s retina. He nearly lost his sight, and he knows what it is to be the frightened patient your staff meet every day. His eyesight recovered. His relationship with perception did not.

Six years later he found Dr. John Demartini’s research into how perception drives reaction and became a Trained Demartini Method Facilitator. He is President-Elect of the National Speakers Association, Southern California, and its 2026 Chapter Member of the Year. Originally from Perth, Australia, he is based in Los Angeles and travels to speak.
