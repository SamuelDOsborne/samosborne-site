Clarifying the Value

What NSA SoCal is — and what it is not.

One of the biggest conversion barriers is misunderstanding the chapter’s role. The website needs to clarify the model immediately and positively.

Misconception

“NSA gets speakers gigs.”

  • Creates the wrong expectation for first-time visitors
  • Frames the chapter like a bureau or agency
  • Undercuts the real value of the community
Correct Positioning

“NSA helps speakers build the business that gets them booked.”

  • Develop your speaking craft and marketability
  • Strengthen positioning, systems, and visibility
  • Build relationships that lead to real opportunities

Questions Speakers Ask

This gives the homepage sharper segmentation and helps visitors immediately see where they fit.

Emerging Speakers
