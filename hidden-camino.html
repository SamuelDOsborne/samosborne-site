What You Receive

The full offering.

Every element is designed to work together. The walk does not decorate the facilitation. The facilitation does not pause for the walk. They are one process.

Daily
Morning Walk in Silence
Each day opens with a question given at the trailhead. You carry it as you walk. No phones. No conversation. Just the question, the coast, and the movement of your body through space. By the time you arrive, something has already shifted.
Daily
Midday Teaching
A short teaching drawn from the Demartini Method, Jungian depth psychology, and the hermetic tradition — rendered in language your leadership can use on Monday morning. Each teaching maps to the terrain you walked that day. The landscape becomes the lesson.
Daily
Evening Facilitation
Group and individual Demartini Method facilitation. Specific people. Specific charges. Dissolved in real time — with witnesses. Physical exhaustion has already lowered your defences. The evening is where transformation becomes visible. You will see it happen in others. You will feel it happen in yourself.
Daily
Integration at the Table
Dinner together. Local wine. The kind of conversation that only happens when the distractions have been walked away. No agenda. No performance. Just eight people in northern Spain who have spent the day doing something real.
Closing
90-Day Application Plan
You do not leave with a memory. You leave with a documented perceptual shift for each relationship you worked on, and a concrete 90-day plan for applying the method in your leadership environment. The Camino ends. The work continues.
Ongoing
Alumni Circle
Hidden Camino alumni join a closed cohort that meets quarterly — virtually — for continued facilitation, peer accountability, and deepening application of the Demartini Method. Sam facilitates each session. The eight people who walked together do not simply go home. They remain a working group. Membership is for life, at no additional fee.

Narrative

Sam was pulled in two directions as a kid. One was outward — toward space, toward what governs it, what holds it together. The other was inward — toward the patterns of human perception, emotion, and behaviour. He didn't know then that those were the same question.

Before the keynote stage, he was a school teacher, a professional beatboxer, a DJ, a living statue performer. He has held the attention of every kind of audience, under every kind of condition. He knows what it means to be physically present in something uncomfortable and find the order inside it.

He discovered the Demartini Method in 2014. Over the decade that followed, he attended the Breakthrough Experience multiple times — including April 2026. He has studied under John Demartini and is pursuing Master Facilitator designation. The Hidden Camino is the place where his deepest intellectual and physical interests converge: the hidden order of the psyche, revealed through the hidden order of an ancient road.

He will walk every kilometre with you. He will facilitate every evening. He will not ask you to go anywhere he has not been.

Express Your Interest

Who this is for

Leaders in high-stakes roles — financial services, legal, healthcare, executive leadership — where specific relationships trigger reactivity that undermines composure, distorts judgment, or erodes credibility. People who have already done enough personal development to know that insight alone is not the limiting factor. The method is.

What this is not

Not a team building exercise. Not a fitness challenge. Not spiritual tourism. The Camino del Norte is one of Europe's most demanding coastal walks. You will be physically tired every evening. That is not incidental — it is the mechanism.

Physical preparation

Participants should be capable of walking 20–35 km per day on varied terrain. A preparation guide is provided upon acceptance. No hiking experience required — only the willingness to be physically present in what is uncomfortable.

Corporate sponsorship

Organisations may sponsor a participant's place. An invoice can be issued to your organisation. Indicate this in your application and we will accommodate the arrangement in the consultation call.
