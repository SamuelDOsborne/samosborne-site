Why This Work Exists

Most problems in high-stakes work are not strategic first. They are perceptual.

You do not lose composure because the stakes are high. You lose it because specific people distort how you see, interpret, and respond. This work exists to address that distortion.

The Problem This Solves

Specific people are costing you composure.

Not in theory. In depositions, negotiations, and boardrooms. In how much authority you can actually access when the room turns hostile.

The Adversary

Opposing counsel, the counterparty, the negotiator across the table. Their provocation is deliberate — and it works. You leave still arguing with them in your head, and your strategy narrows to a reaction.

The Client

They ignore your counsel, then hold you responsible for the outcome. Resentment creeps into the relationship, and your judgment stops being clean.

The Boss or Senior Partner

Their scrutiny changes your posture. You over-prepare, second-guess, and start performing competence instead of exercising it.

The Board

Every meeting becomes a firing squad. One member's hostility triggers your defensiveness before the agenda even starts — and the whole room reads it.

The Rival

A peer competing for the same ground — the promotion, the account, the mandate. Their presence pulls you off your own game and into theirs.

The Direct Report

Their resistance pulls you toward micromanagement, avoidance, or frustration. Delegation becomes distorted by charge, and the work suffers for it.

Sequential Facilitation

Each session applies the Demartini Method to a different charged relationship in your work. One session for the adversary across the table. One for the client who won't take counsel. One for the partner, boss, or board member whose presence changes your posture the moment they enter the room.

This is not generic professional development. It is precision work applied to the relationships where emotional charge is quietly distorting judgment, advocacy, and authority.

Common Questions

What to expect.

How is this different from executive coaching?▼

Executive coaching usually develops skills, habits, or strategy. This work addresses the emotional charge distorting how you perceive specific people. You are not learning techniques to manage difficult relationships — you are working on how those relationships are held in perception.

Is this therapy or mental health treatment?▼

No. The Demartini Method is a structured, educational facilitation process, not psychotherapy or counseling. It does not diagnose or treat any medical, psychological, or mental health condition, and it is not a substitute for care from a licensed mental health professional. If you are dealing with a mental health condition, please work with a qualified provider — this work can sit alongside that care, not replace it.

What if I need more than 8 or 16 sessions?▼

Most clients find that 8–16 facilitations address the relationships carrying the most charge. Where the ecosystem is broader, the Strategic engagement extends the work across a full year. The aim is not ongoing dependency — it is focused work, scoped to your reality.

How quickly will I see results?▼

Many clients report a noticeable shift in how they hold the relationship during or shortly after a session. Individual experiences vary, and no specific result or timeframe is guaranteed. The practical test is how the relationship feels the next time you walk into that courtroom, deposition, negotiation, or boardroom.

What happens during a session?▼

Each session is a structured facilitation. You identify the person and the trait carrying charge, and I guide you through a precise sequence of questions designed to reveal where that same trait appears in your own form and to bring your perception of that person into balance.

How is confidentiality handled?▼

Sessions are private, and your information is kept confidential under the terms of our written engagement agreement. To be clear, particularly for a legal audience: this is not a legally privileged relationship. Communications with a facilitator are not protected by attorney–client, therapist–patient, or any similar privilege, and are not shielded from subpoena or discovery. For that reason, the work is deliberately structured so you never need to share case specifics, privileged material, confidential business information, or client identities — the method operates on your perception of the person, not the facts of the matter.

Is this appropriate for firms or teams?▼

This advisory is designed for individual professionals. Firm-wide, practice group, team, or association applications require a different engagement structure. Reach out if you want to discuss that separately.

Do I need to have attended one of your keynotes?▼

No. Many advisory clients first encountered the work during a keynote, but the engagement is open to any professional dealing with charge toward specific people.
