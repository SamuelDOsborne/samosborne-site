Common Questions

What to expect.

How is this different from executive coaching?▼

Executive coaching usually develops skills, habits, or strategy. This work dissolves the emotional charge distorting how you perceive specific people. You are not learning techniques to manage difficult relationships. You are transforming the way those relationships are held in perception.

What if I need more than 8 or 16 sessions?▼

Most leaders find that 8–16 facilitations address the relationships carrying the most charge. For leaders whose ecosystem is broader or whose role demands ongoing partnership, the Strategic engagement extends the work across a full year. The aim is not ongoing dependency. The aim is clean transformation, scoped to your reality.

How quickly will I see results?▼

The shift happens during the session itself. You will usually feel the relationship change immediately. The real proof comes the next time you encounter that person and notice the charge is no longer running the interaction.

What happens during a session?▼

Each session is a structured facilitation. You identify the person and the trait carrying charge. I guide you through precise questions that reveal where that same trait appears in your own form. As perception balances, the emotional charge around that person collapses.

Is this appropriate for organizational teams?▼

This advisory is designed for individual leaders. Team, board, or organizational applications require a different engagement structure. Reach out if you want to discuss that separately.

Do I need to have attended one of your keynotes?▼

No. Many advisory clients first encountered the work during a keynote, but the engagement is open to any leader dealing with charge toward specific people that is undermining their authority or clarity.
