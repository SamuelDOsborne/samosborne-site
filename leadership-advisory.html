How is this different from executive coaching?▼

Executive coaching typically focuses on skill development, behavioral modification, or goal achievement. This is perceptual transformation. The Demartini Method dissolves the emotional charge at the root of reactivity. You don't learn techniques to manage emotions around difficult people. You permanently transform how you perceive them. What if I need more than 8 or 16 sessions?▼

Most leaders find that 8–16 facilitations address their highest-priority charged relationships. If additional sessions are required, we can discuss extension options. The goal is permanent transformation, not ongoing dependency. Once you've experienced the method systematically applied across your key relationships, the perceptual pattern becomes self-evident.

How quickly will I see results?▼

Perceptual balance occurs during the facilitation session itself. You'll notice the shift immediately — the person who previously triggered intense reactivity no longer carries the same emotional charge. The composure you demonstrate in your next interaction with them confirms the transformation. The charge dissolves when perception balances.

What happens during a session?▼

Each one-hour session is a structured facilitation. You identify the specific person and the trait that triggers emotional charge. I guide you through precision questions that reveal where you display that same trait in your own form. Through reflective awareness and perceptual balance equations, the projection mechanics dissolve. By the end of the session, the emotional charge around that person is gone.

This advisory service is for individual leaders. If you're interested in applying the Demartini Method to team dynamics, organizational conflict, or leadership group facilitation, that requires a different engagement structure. Contact me to discuss organizational consulting options.
